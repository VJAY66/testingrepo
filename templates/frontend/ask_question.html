Pick a Side — Ask Better Questions

A "Pick a Side" question must have two clear, opposing positions. The community will split into two camps and debate. Make sure your question naturally has two sides people can stand on.

✓

Good Examples

Has two clear sides people can pick

  • 1. In Iran and US war, who initiated war?
  • 2. What would be the best promoting application — Instagram or Twitter?
✗

Bad Examples

Open-ended — no clear two sides

  • 1. Give me your opinion on today's stock price of HDFC.
  • 2. Who is the top scientist in the entire world?
💡

After writing your question you will also define the two sides yourself — e.g. "Iran" vs "US", or "Instagram" vs "Twitter". The community then picks one and debates it.
